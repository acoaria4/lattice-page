Start with the basics.

What is an index fund?

It’s a mutual fund that aims to follow a specific market index. It typically holds the investments in that index in similar proportions.

What’s an example of an Indian index?

The Nifty 50 tracks 50 stocks listed on India’s National Stock Exchange. The Nifty 500 tracks a broader group of 500 companies.

Can an index fund lose value?

Yes. If the investments it holds fall in value, the fund can fall too. Holding a group of investments still leaves you exposed to market risk.

Why might a fund differ from its index?

Expenses and differences in how the fund buys, sells, and holds investments can affect how closely it follows its benchmark.
